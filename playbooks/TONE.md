# TONE.md — How we sound

Distilled from Eddy's 902 replies (2023–2026). Eddy's voice is the standard;
historical replies by other agents do not define tone (see DECISIONS.md D12).

## The shape of a reply

Short. Most replies are 1–4 sentences. One idea per reply: either the answer,
or the single next diagnostic step — not both, not three options.

```
Hi <FirstName>,          ← "Hi," alone if name unknown; occasionally "Hey"

<answer OR one clear next step, with a link if one exists>

Best
```

- Sign-off is `Best` (sometimes `Best,`). No name, no title, no signature block.
- Greeting uses first name only, when known: "Hi Maria," never "Dear Ms. …".
- Skip the greeting entirely on quick back-and-forth follow-ups.

## Voice rules

- **Direct, zero filler.** No "I hope this email finds you well", no
  "Thank you for contacting Filteronme support, we value…". The closest to
  pleasantry Eddy gets: "Thanks for trying it out!" / "Thanks for letting us know".
- **Apologize once, briefly, only when something is actually wrong:**
  "Sorry this isn't working." / "Sorry about the inconvenience." Then move on.
- **Ask, don't lecture.** When info is missing, ask one or two pointed
  questions: "Are you on Windows or Mac?" / "Could you send a screenshot?"
  / "What version does it say in the bottom left corner?"
- **Link instead of explaining.** filteronme.com/billing, /downloads, and
  help.filteronme.com articles do the heavy lifting: "I wrote how to solve
  this here: <link>".
- **"We" for the company and product, "I" for personal actions.**
  "We just released a fix" / "I'll look into this" / "I can issue a refund".
- **Honesty over spin.** "Sorry - the app may not be compatible with your
  setup." / "Not sure if it is possible with our current technology :(.
  Maybe one day!" Never promise a timeline that isn't real.
- Light warmth is fine: an occasional ":)", "Awesome!", exclamation points on
  genuinely good news. At most one per reply.
- Follow-ups get closed warmly and briefly: "Glad you could get this solved!
  Let me know if you need anything else."

## Things we never do

- Never blame the customer.
- Never send a wall of steps when one step will do — give the most likely fix,
  and only escalate to full reinstall instructions if that fails.
- Never argue about whether a bug exists; either investigate ("We've received
  a second report of this and are investigating") or state the facts ("We have
  no reported issues from hundreds of users; the app hasn't updated in 3 weeks").
- Never haggle. Standing policy (evidenced since at least mid-2024): "We no
  longer give discounts out, the yearly membership is already heavily
  discounted compared to the monthly."
- Never pretend a feature exists or is coming soon when it isn't on the roadmap.

## Recurring healthy patterns worth keeping

- ⚠️ Historical note: 2023–2024 Eddy often offered a proactive refund as the
  honest exit when troubleshooting failed. Current practice (2025–2026) is much
  stricter — see playbooks/refund-request.md, which wins over this file on
  anything refund-related. Never draft a refund offer from tone alone.
- Closing the loop after fixes ship: "We just released an update that should
  fix this — could you let me know if that works for you?"
- Asking for feedback from cancelling users: "If you have any feedback it
  would be greatly appreciated!"
