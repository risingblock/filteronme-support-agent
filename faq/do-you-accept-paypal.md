We don't accept PayPal. Checkout runs through Stripe, which offers several payment methods depending on where you're buying from — major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Stripe Link, Amazon Pay, and Cash App. SEPA direct debit shows up only for customers checking out from Austria or Germany.

Not seeing the option you expected? Try signing out of Stripe Link first — it can hide Apple Pay and Google Pay. See [How to pay with GCash](how-to-pay-with-gcash) for the sign-out steps.
