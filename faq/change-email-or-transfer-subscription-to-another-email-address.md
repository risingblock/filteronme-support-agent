Go to [filteronme.com/change-email](https://www.filteronme.com/change-email) to move your subscription to a new email address yourself.

You'll need access to both inboxes: we send a verification code to your **old** email first to confirm it's really you, then a second code to the **new** email to confirm you own that one too. Only once both are verified does the subscription move.

This only works for an active subscription. If you no longer have access to the old email, use the [lost access to email](lost-access-to-email) flow instead.

Still stuck? Email support@filteronme.com
