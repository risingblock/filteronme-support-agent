The free version keeps a watermark permanently — to remove it, upgrade to premium.

Click **Upgrade** in the bottom-left of the app.

![screenshot](images/116-how-to-remove-watermark-by-upgrading/2.jpg)

Already paid and still seeing the watermark? That's usually a login mismatch, not a billing problem — see [restore purchase or access existing subscription](restore-purchase-or-access-existing-subscription).
