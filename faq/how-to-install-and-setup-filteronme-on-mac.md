Filteronme for Mac needs **macOS 13 or later, on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)**. Intel Macs aren't supported. On macOS 12? See [the old version](download-old-mac-version).

On an older Mac, or macOS 12.4–12.x, the current app won't run — use [our last compatible build](download-old-mac-version) instead.

## Install

1. Download Filteronme from [filteronme.com/download-mac](https://www.filteronme.com/download-mac).
2. Drag Filteronme into your **Applications** folder. This step matters — the camera driver won't register if you run the app from the installer window or your Desktop.

![screenshot](images/99-how-to-install-and-setup-filteronme-on-mac/2.gif)

3. Open Filteronme from Spotlight Search or your Applications folder.
4. Allow camera access when macOS asks.
5. Approve the camera extension: open **System Settings → Login Items & Extensions → Camera Extensions**, click the info icon next to Filteronme.

![screenshot](images/99-how-to-install-and-setup-filteronme-on-mac/3.png)

6. Toggle the Filteronme camera extension on.

![screenshot](images/99-how-to-install-and-setup-filteronme-on-mac/4.jpg)

Can't find this panel, or on an older macOS? See [where is the camera extension in Mac settings](where-is-camera-extension-in-mac-settings).

7. Sign in with your email.
8. Pick the camera you want to use inside Filteronme.
9. In your video app (Zoom, Discord, FaceTime, Meet, etc.), open its video/camera settings and select **Filteronme Camera**.

![screenshot](images/99-how-to-install-and-setup-filteronme-on-mac/5.jpg)
