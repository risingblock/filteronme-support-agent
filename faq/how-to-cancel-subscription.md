Easiest way: click the **Billing** button in the app. It logs you straight into the billing portal — no separate login needed.

![screenshot](images/84-how-to-cancel-subscription/2.jpg)

From there you can cancel or manage your plan.

![screenshot](images/84-how-to-cancel-subscription/3.jpg)

No Billing button, or cancelling from a browser? Go to https://filteronme.com/billing and log in with the email your receipt was sent to. If you paid with Apple Pay or Google Pay, that's often a different email than the one you're writing from — try a few, or use filteronme.com/billing-email to have a login link sent to the right one.

On the free trial? There's nothing to cancel. Trials don't need a card and expire on their own.

Charge look unfamiliar? See [What is this FILTERONME.COM charge?](charge-on-your-statement) before assuming something's wrong.
