WhatsApp support is desktop-app only — the WhatsApp mobile app can't use Filteronme.

Open Filteronme first, then select **Filteronme Camera** during a video call in the WhatsApp desktop app.

![screenshot](images/137-whatsapp/2.jpg)

Getting a "Camera In Use" error on Windows? Open Filteronme first and confirm your camera shows up in its preview, then open WhatsApp.

Still not working? Try the fallback chain: your camera → Filteronme → OBS → WhatsApp. Add Filteronme as a source in OBS, then select the "OBS Virtual Camera" inside WhatsApp.

Still stuck? Email support@filteronme.com
