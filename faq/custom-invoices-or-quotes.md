All your receipts and invoices live in the billing portal — click **Billing** in the app, or go to https://filteronme.com/billing.

![screenshot](images/120-custom-invoices-or-quotes/2.jpg)

We don't customize invoices — no VAT numbers, no company letterhead, no reverse-charge notes added on our end. We're a US company and don't have a VAT number to add in the first place. Download the PDF from the portal and add whatever you need yourself with Preview, Acrobat, or a free tool like Smallpdf.

Same answer for custom quotes or pro forma invoices for procurement: pay normally, then edit the resulting invoice PDF.

Still stuck? Email support@filteronme.com
