Open Filteronme first, then open Discord — Filteronme needs to already be running before Discord looks for cameras.

Select **Filteronme** as the camera inside Discord's video settings.

- **Windows:** keep Filteronme open the whole time.
- **Mac (latest version):** once the camera is selected in Discord, you can close Filteronme to save resources.

Depending on your version of Discord, the Filteronme camera may not list directly. If that happens, the fallback chain that works is:

Filteronme → OBS → Discord

Set Filteronme as your source in OBS, then select the OBS virtual camera inside Discord.
