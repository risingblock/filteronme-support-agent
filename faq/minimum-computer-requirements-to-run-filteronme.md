**Mac:** the current version needs **macOS 13 or later**, on **Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)**. Intel Macs aren't supported — there's no workaround for that.

On an older Mac, or macOS 12.4–12.x, use [our last compatible build](download-old-mac-version) instead.

**Windows:** at least **8GB of RAM** and a **~3.8GHz i3 or better**. We've tested successfully on a budget machine (Acer Aspire 3 A314-36P-35UU), so you don't need a high-end PC.

Not sure it'll run well on your machine? Use the free trial to test it — no card required.
