A full reinstall fixes most Mac problems and is required for most upgrades.

1. Open FilterOnMe.
2. Click **Advanced** in the top-left menu, then **Uninstall Extension**. (2.1 and later only — skip this step if you're on an older version.)

![screenshot](images/123-how-to-do-a-full-reinstall-of-filteronme-for-mac/2.jpg)

3. Confirm the removal if you're asked to.

![screenshot](images/123-how-to-do-a-full-reinstall-of-filteronme-for-mac/3.jpg)

4. Delete FilterOnMe from your **Applications** folder.
5. Reboot your computer.
6. Open Zoom, Discord, or FaceTime and confirm **"FilterOnMe Camera"** no longer shows up in the camera list. If it still does, the old extension didn't fully clear — reboot again before continuing.
7. Download the latest version from [filteronme.com/download-mac](https://www.filteronme.com/download-mac).
8. Reinstall — follow the [Mac install guide](how-to-install-and-setup-filteronme-on-mac).

On an old Mac that can't run the current version, use the [old Mac build](download-old-mac-version) instead of step 7.
