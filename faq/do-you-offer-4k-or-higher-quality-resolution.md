We don't support 4K — by design, not because it's still on the roadmap. It's too resource-intensive for most people's computers, and most video call apps (Zoom, Meet, Teams, etc.) recompress your video anyway, so the real-world quality gain would be minimal.

Current output resolution:

- **Mac:** recent versions (2.1+) support 1080p output.
- **Windows:** capped at 720p.

If your video looks lower-quality than expected, make sure you're on the latest version — [filteronme.com/downloads](https://filteronme.com/downloads).
