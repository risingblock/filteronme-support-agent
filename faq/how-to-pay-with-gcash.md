We don't accept GCash directly, but a GCash card usually works fine as a regular card at checkout.

Card not going through? See [GCash's guide to card declines](https://help.gcash.com/hc/en-us/articles/30287172161305-My-GCash-Card-is-not-being-accepted-by-a-merchant-for-payment-What-do-I-do).

We also accept major credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Stripe Link. Not seeing Apple Pay or Google Pay at checkout? [Sign out of Stripe Link](https://support.link.com/questions/how-do-i-log-out-of-link-on-all-devices) first — it can hide those options. Many customers find adding their card to Link, then paying at checkout, works best.
