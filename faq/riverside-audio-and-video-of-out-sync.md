Audio/video sync issues on Riverside are usually a Riverside or hardware timing issue, not something Filteronme causes directly — Filteronme only supplies the video feed.

A couple of resources that may help:

- [Riverside's own guide to syncing audio and video](https://riverside.fm/blog/how-to-sync-audio-video-sources)
