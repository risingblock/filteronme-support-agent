There's nothing to cancel — the free trial doesn't need a credit card and expires on its own.

- The trial lasts 7 days and starts the moment you first log in, which for almost everyone is the same moment you create your account.
- We never collect payment info for the trial. If you didn't upgrade to premium, there's no card on file and nothing that can charge you.
- Once the 7 days are up, it just ends — no action needed from you.

If you did upgrade to a paid subscription, that's a different flow — see [how to cancel subscription](how-to-cancel-subscription).
