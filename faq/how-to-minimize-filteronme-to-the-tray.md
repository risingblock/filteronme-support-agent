**Mac (latest version):** FilterOnMe doesn't need to stay open once your camera is selected elsewhere — just close it.

**Windows:** there's no built-in tray-minimize. A third-party tool like RBTray can do this for you. RBTray isn't ours — we don't control or support it and can't guarantee it'll keep working.
