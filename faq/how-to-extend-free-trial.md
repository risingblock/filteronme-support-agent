We don't extend free trials as a general rule. Please consider subscribing — see [how to remove watermark by upgrading](how-to-remove-watermark-by-upgrading).

The one exception: if something on **our** side kept you from actually using the trial — a bug, or an installer that wouldn't work — email support@filteronme.com and we'll take a look. Being busy, not being ready, or wanting a second look before buying doesn't qualify.

Trials also reset on their own after a couple months, so if you're not in a hurry you can just wait and try again then.

Still stuck? Email support@filteronme.com
