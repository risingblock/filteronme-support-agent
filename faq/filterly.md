Filteronme.com is not affiliated with Filterly. If you're trying to cancel a Filterly subscription, you'll need to contact Filterly's support directly — we can't access or cancel it.

Not sure which one charged you? Check your receipt or card statement. Our charges always show as **WWW.FILTERONME.COM** or **Filteronme**. If yours says **Filterly** (or a close misspelling like Fiterly, Filtery, or Filtermi), that's them, not us.

To manage your Filteronme subscription instead, go to https://filteronme.com/billing.

See also: [What is this FILTERONME.COM charge on my statement?](charge-on-your-statement)

Still stuck? Email support@filteronme.com
