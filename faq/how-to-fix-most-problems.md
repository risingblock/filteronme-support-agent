Work through these in order — most tickets are solved by the first two or three steps.

1. **Open Filteronme before you open the app you're using it in** (Zoom, OBS, Discord, etc.) — the other app needs to find the Filteronme camera already running. Windows: keep Filteronme open for the whole call. Mac 2.1 and later: you can close Filteronme again once you've selected the camera.

2. **Switch your camera selection to something else and back**, or unplug and replug your physical camera.

![screenshot](images/105-how-to-fix-most-problems/2.jpg)

3. **Reboot the Filteronme app** — quit it fully and reopen it.

4. **Mac only: toggle camera permission off and back on** for Filteronme in System Settings. Still stuck? Do the same with the camera extension toggle.

![screenshot](images/105-how-to-fix-most-problems/3.png)

5. **Reinstall the camera extension.** In Filteronme, go to the top-left menu → Advanced → Uninstall Extension, then reopen Filteronme and reinstall it.

6. **Restart your computer.**

7. **None of that worked? Do a full reinstall.** Mac steps: [how to do a full reinstall of Filteronme for Mac](how-to-do-a-full-reinstall-of-filteronme-for-mac). On Windows, download the latest installer from [filteronme.com/downloads](https://filteronme.com/downloads) and reinstall.

Still stuck? Email support@filteronme.com
